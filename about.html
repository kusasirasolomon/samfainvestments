Our Story

Built on Trust, Driven by Excellence

Samfa Investments Limited was established with a clear vision: to become Uganda's most trusted multi-sector business partner. From our headquarters in Iganga, we serve clients across the entire country with dedication, transparency, and consistently high-quality products and services.

Over the years, we have expanded our portfolio to cover six key sectors — stationery, office supplies, catering, agro products, electronics & electrical, and cosmology. This diversity allows us to serve schools, hospitals, government agencies, NGOs, SMEs, and individual households under one trusted brand.

Every product we source, every service we offer, and every client relationship we build reflects our core commitment: delivering real value in every transaction.
